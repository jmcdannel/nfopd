Forecasts: NOAA National Weather Service grid forecast, 7-day forecast, discussions, alerts and radar; NOAA NBM, HRRR and GFS plus ECMWF, GEM and ICON via Open-Meteo. Observations: USDA NRCS SNOTEL and NWS stations. Avalanche danger: avalanche.org centers. Snow reports and webcams come from each resort's own website, linked on its page.

Model snowfall is worked out hour by hour from each model's liquid precipitation at the mountain's elevation. NWS snowfall is shown as issued. How NFOPD calculates its forecasts.
